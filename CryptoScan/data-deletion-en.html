Principle

CryptoScan creates no account and operates no server that stores your data. All information entered in the app (public addresses, labels, balance history, preferences, optional API keys) remains exclusively in the local database on your device.

As a result: deletion is immediate and complete. Simply erase the app's data on your device. No remote copy exists.

Delete local data

  • In the app: open Settings, then use the "Reset" button. The entire local SQLite database is instantly destroyed.
  • From Android: Settings → Apps → CryptoScan → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.

Advertising data

Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads. You can limit or delete the advertising identifiers used at any time:

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • Withdraw consent: in the app, Settings → Privacy preferences (GDPR). The Google UMP form lets you withdraw granted consent.
  • Google policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Email request

To submit an erasure request or exercise your GDPR rights (access, rectification, portability, deletion), write to:

matisse.demontis@proton.me

Use the subject "Data deletion request". Since the publisher holds no personal data, the reply will confirm that no information about you is retained. Response time: 30 days maximum.

What is retained

  • By the publisher: no user data, no identifier, no history.
  • By Google Mobile Ads: technical logs related to ad delivery follow Google's retention policy, independent of the publisher. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • On your device: only the backup files you exported yourself (.enc format). They are under your exclusive control.
