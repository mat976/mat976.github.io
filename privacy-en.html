2. Nature of the service (watch-only / read-only)

CryptoScan is a technical tool that displays, aggregates and tracks public data from distributed ledgers (blockchains).

  • No private keys: the app never generates, requests, handles or stores any private key, recovery phrase (seed phrase) or wallet password.
  • No financial transactions: the app cannot send, transfer, deposit, swap or withdraw crypto-assets or fiat currency.
  • No financial advice: all data, charts, prices and simulations are provided for technical and informational purposes only. They do not constitute investment advice or a regulated financial service under the EU MiCA regulation.

3. Personal data processing & GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679)

3.1. Data stored locally on your device

  • Data categories: public blockchain addresses entered by the user, custom labels, past balance snapshots, display preferences.
  • Storage: confined exclusively to the sandboxed local SQLite database on your smartphone.
  • Access: none of this data is transmitted to the publisher or to any proprietary server. The publisher has no access to your wallet.

3.2. Direct network communications (RPC nodes and explorers)

To query the balances of the public addresses you watch, your device directly contacts the following endpoints:

  • Bitcoin: mempool.space, blockstream.info
  • Ethereum / EVM: 1rpc.io, ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com, cloudflare-eth.com
  • Solana: api.mainnet-beta.solana.com, solana-rpc.publicnode.com
  • Bittensor: lite.chain.opentensor.ai, api.taostats.io
  • Kaspa: api.kaspa.org
  • Market prices: api.coingecko.com
Privacy note: with each HTTPS request, your public IP address is transmitted to the remote third-party servers for technical routing requirements, in accordance with those providers' privacy policies. You may use a VPN or configure your own private RPC nodes in Settings to preserve your network anonymity.

3.3. Monetization & advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

The app embeds the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

  • Data processed by Google: device technical identifiers (Google Advertising ID / IDFA), approximate IP address (for regional geo-targeting), ad interaction data and technical diagnostics.
  • Consent collection (CMP / IAB TCF v2.2): in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the app uses the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). No advertising tracker is activated without your prior consent.
  • Managing your choices: you can modify or revoke your advertising consent at any time from the app's Settings screen via the "Privacy preferences (GDPR)" button.

4. Legal basis for processing (Art. 6 GDPR)

  1. Performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): local processing of public addresses to display your portfolio.
  2. Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): storage of identifiers and delivery of targeted advertising via Google AdMob.
  3. Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): ad fraud prevention and technical security of the application.

5. Backups & cryptographic security (Art. 32 GDPR)

  • The app lets you export your backups in encrypted .enc format.
  • The algorithm used is the international standard AES-256-GCM, with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation (100,000 iterations) and a random 16-byte cryptographic salt.
  • Export compliance: compliant with US EAR export regulations (Mass Market Category 5D992.c) and declared to the competent authorities (ANSSI in France).

6. Your rights (GDPR Articles 15 to 22)

Under European regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, port and delete your data:

  • Full deletion: by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset" button in Settings, all of your local SQLite data is instantly destroyed with no remote copy.
  • Complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in France: CNIL — www.cnil.fr).

Investing in crypto-assets carries a high risk of capital loss due to extreme market volatility. Past performance and variation simulations (staking / dTAO) are purely indicative and do not guarantee future results.
